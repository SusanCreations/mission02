Love your skin with Forever Beauty

CHRISTCHURCH SKIN HEALTH AND COSMETIC TATTOOING

At Forever Beauty, we bring together skilled hands, modern techniques and a love of skincare to help you look and feel your best. From dermal needling and anti-ageing facials to acne and scar care, permanent makeup, waxing and tinting, every treatment is tailored to refresh, restore and bring vitality back to your skin.

Our Treatments

Facials

Our professional facials use O Cosmedics, an Australian-made cosmedical skincare range trusted by skin experts. Every facial starts with a skin analysis, then a tailored peel and calming mask chosen for your skin, from a gentle glow-boosting treatment to deeper resurfacing. Facials are the foundation of our anti-ageing and acne programmes.

Dermal Needling

Using the Dermapen 4, we create thousands of tiny channels in the skin to kick-start your body’s natural healing response and boost collagen and elastin. It’s a quick, non-surgical treatment that helps soften fine lines, refine enlarged pores and improve the look of scarring. Expect some redness for a short time afterwards, with smoother, plumper-looking skin to follow.

Anti-ageing

Our anti-ageing programme combines O Cosmedics facials with Dermapen dermal needling for results from the surface down. Facials hydrate, brighten and resurface, while needling stimulates new collagen to firm and smooth. Together they help reduce fine lines and wrinkles and restore a fresh, youthful glow.

Acne & Scar Care

Clearer skin starts with a plan. We pair targeted O Cosmedics facials, which calm breakouts, rebalance oil and fade post-acne marks, with Dermapen dermal needling, which has a dedicated scar setting to improve the look of acne scars. Your therapist will build a treatment and home-care plan around your skin.

Cosmetic Tattooing

Wake up ready with permanent makeup tailored to your face. We offer brows (microblading and powder brows), lip blush and lash line enhancement for natural-looking definition that saves you time every day. Every treatment begins with a consultation to design your shape and colour.

Brow Sculpting

Frame your face with brows shaped to suit you. We map, wax, tweeze and trim to create your ideal shape, then finish with a custom tint for fuller, more defined brows. Add a lash tint for a fresh, mascara-free look.
