Dermal Needling

Dermapen dermal needling is one of the most effective non-surgical treatments for renewing your skin. Using the Dermapen 4, we create thousands of tiny channels in the skin, which trigger your body's natural healing response to produce fresh collagen and elastin. The result is smoother, firmer, clearer-looking skin, achieved with minimal downtime.

What it treats

Dermal needling can help with fine lines and wrinkles, loss of firmness, dull skin, redness, pigmentation and sun damage, acne scarring, thick and congested skin, enlarged pores and uneven texture. See our Anti-Ageing and Acne & Scar Care treatments for more about these programmes.

Not just for acne scars

What many people don't know is how effective dermal needling can be for all kinds of scars, on both the face and body. Whether it's a surgical scar, an old injury or stretch marks, needling encourages fresh collagen to rebuild and remodel the skin. Over a course of treatments, scars can become softer, smoother and much less noticeable.

Downtime and aftercare

Expect some redness and sensitivity, similar to mild sunburn, which usually settles within 24 hours. Some light flaking may follow over the next few days. Your Skin Expert will recommend the right home care to support your skin while it heals and help your results last.

How many sessions?

Most clients see the best results with a course of 2 to 8 sessions, depending on their skin concern. Results continue to build in the weeks after each treatment, as your skin produces new collagen.

Cosmetic Tattooing

Wake up ready, every day. Cosmetic tattooing enhances your natural features with results that last, from a soft, barely-there look to a defined, made-up finish. Every treatment begins with a consultation, where we design your shape and colour together so your result suits your face, your style and your lifestyle.

Brows

Microblading: fine, hand-drawn hair strokes that mimic real brow hairs for a soft, natural look.

Nano Brows: ultra-fine hair strokes created with a machine, for crisp, realistic detail that suits most skin types.

Ombre Brows: a soft, powdered effect that fades from lighter at the front to more defined at the tail, like a filled-in brow.

Combination Brows: hair strokes at the front with soft shading through the body and tail, giving the best of both.

Eyeliner

Lash Line Enhancement: pigment placed between the lashes to make them look thicker and fuller, with no obvious line.

Classic Eyeliner: a defined line along the lash line, from fine to bold, on the top or bottom lid, or both.

Ombre Eyeliner: a soft, smoky liner that blends outwards for a diffused, sultry look.

Lips

Lip Blush: a sheer wash of colour that enhances your natural lip tone, like a tinted lip gloss finish.

Lip Liner: a sharp line that defines and shapes the edge of your lips for a crisp, even outline.

Lip Liner & Blend: a defined liner softly blended inwards for added shape and fullness.

Full Lip Colour: richer, all-over colour for a polished, lipstick-like finish that lasts.

Brow Sculpting

Not ready for cosmetic tattooing? Brow sculpting is a gentle, non-invasive way to transform your brows, with no healing time. With the right shaping, tinting, henna or lamination, you can achieve beautifully full, defined brows and walk straight back into your day.

Brow Shape & Tint

Frame your face with brows shaped to suit you. We map, wax, tweeze and trim to create your ideal shape, then finish with a custom tint for fuller, more defined brows. Add a lash tint for a fresh, mascara-free look.

Henna Brows

Henna tints both the brow hairs and the skin underneath, creating a soft, powdery fill that makes sparse brows look fuller. It's a great way to try a more defined look, and it lasts longer than a standard tint.

Brow Lamination

Think of it as a perm for your brows. Lamination sets your brow hairs into a fuller, fluffy, brushed-up shape that stays in place. It's perfect for thin or patchy brows, and for taming unruly hairs that grow in different directions. Lamination can retrain them to sit neatly where you want them.
